# Fishy privacy policy

Fishy does not collect, share or sell any data about you or your cat.

- **No data collected.** Fishy has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- **No internet.** The iPhone, iPad and Apple TV apps never connect to the internet.
- **Touches stay on the screen.** Taps are used only to tell whether a paw caught the fish. Nothing is recorded or stored.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/colinbm/fishy/issues.

Last updated 25 November 2026.
